Red-color interest—

The interest meter responds to red-colored pixels, not biological identification. Leaves, dirt, rust, clothing, and lighting can create false highlights. Confirm all findings yourself.

Offline use

This camera and photo-processing tool runs locally in Chrome. Visit the page once while online after an update so Joe's Toolbox can cache it, then open it again without internet. Your camera images are processed on your device; this page does not upload them.

Offline features do not include live maps, weather or the separate Netlify Blood Tracker website.

Color filters

Try “Invert background, keep red” in green foliage or “Grayscale, keep red” on busy ground. Use sensitivity to reduce false highlights.
