    How to inspect a room

    Darken the room, turn on the flashlight, and scan slowly near suspicious objects. Look for small red-circled reflections that remain visible from nearby angles, then physically inspect the object safely. Use yellow marks to identify openings worth checking. A clean scan cannot prove that no surveillance device exists.

    Privacy & offline use

    This page performs camera, photo, and sound processing locally in the browser. There are no uploads or accounts. Open it once while online to let Joe's Toolbox cache it for future offline use. Camera, microphone, and Bluetooth permissions are requested only when you start their features.
