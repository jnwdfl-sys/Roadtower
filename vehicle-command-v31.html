Connection and display options

Regeneration monitoring starts after a 10-second warm-up on each Bluetooth connection. Fuel, DEF and basic temperatures load first. Observed regeneration duration and fuel used cover only the portion measured while connected; they are not ECU lifetime totals.

Range is an estimate, not the factory distance-to-empty. The 24-gallon starting setting may differ by vehicle configuration. Do not drive until empty.

Fit mode automatically maximizes text size on the unfolded screen.

Bluetooth pairing is available from the driving display without a vehicle-speed restriction in this app. Only a passenger should operate controls while moving.

Temperature warnings require repeated fresh readings over at least 10 seconds. Fuel and DEF warn at 30%, 25% and 10% as measured. Startup waits for positive measured fuel flow and fresh core readings for at least five seconds; this is evidence of engine operation, not a direct RPM reading. Tire pressures remain unavailable in this scanner integration. Reports only use fresh data and do not confirm the entire vehicle is safe.

Other fluid levels

Coolant level, brake fluid level, washer fluid level and engine oil level are not verified live parameters with this scanner and vehicle. No guessed percentages or extra requests are used. Check the factory warnings and follow the owner’s manual for physical fluid checks. Coolant temperature does not measure coolant level.

Tire pressure monitoring

Requested warning: 40 PSI or below. Live individual tire pressure readings are not supported in this version. No pressure alarm is active. Use the factory tire pressure display and cold inflation pressure on the driver-door placard.

Live sensor report

Copy the actual current readings and sensor availability. No additional Bluetooth queries are made.

DPF soot percentage is not available until a verified LM2-specific reading is identified. The regeneration trigger is not soot percentage.

Observed regeneration history

Only events observed while connected are recorded. An event may have started before connection or ended after disconnect.

Diagnostic informationStandard engine MIL status only; this version does not scan ABS, airbags, or manufacturer-specific codes.

Transmission temperature is an experimental GM Mode 22 reading (221940); verify against the factory display. DEF level and temperature use standard PID 9B, with level fallback PID 85. Other manufacturer-specific temperatures require verified definitions and adapter support.
