Pentecostal foundations

This study emphasizes a personal relationship with Jesus, the authority of Scripture, prayer, Spirit baptism and empowerment, spiritual gifts, healing prayer, holy living, and sharing the gospel.

Pentecostal churches do not all explain every doctrine in the same way. This study draws on common classical Pentecostal themes, without replacing the teaching and pastoral care of your local church.
1 • The gospel

Jesus saves and makes us new

We respond to God's grace through repentance and faith in Jesus Christ, then grow in obedience and love.

Study John 3 ↗
2 • The Holy Spirit

Spirit baptism and empowerment

Classical Pentecostal teaching emphasizes seeking the baptism in the Holy Spirit for empowerment to witness and serve. Many classical Pentecostal churches teach tongues as initial evidence; other Christian traditions differ.

Study Acts 1–2 ↗
3 • Gifts and fruit

Power led by love

Pentecostals affirm spiritual gifts such as prophecy, tongues, interpretation, healing, and discernment, exercised with love, order, and accountability. Fruit of the Spirit matters as much as gifts.

Study the gifts ↗
4 • Holiness

A changed life

Sanctification is the Spirit's ongoing work shaping our decisions, habits, relationships, and love for others.

Study holy living ↗
5 • Faith and healing

Pray, hope, and care

Ask God boldly for healing and comfort while respecting His wisdom. A sick person should never be blamed for lack of healing, and prayer does not replace appropriate healthcare.

Study healing prayer ↗
6 • Worship and witness

Love God and love people

Spirit-filled worship should lead to generosity, service, compassion, church fellowship, and joyful witness to Christ.

Study the early church ↗
