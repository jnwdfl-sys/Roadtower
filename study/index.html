Ethiopian Orthodox study companion

The Ethiopian Orthodox Tewahedo biblical tradition counts 81 books, with distinctive books and historical counting conventions. Its narrower and broader canon descriptions vary. This companion highlights selected texts; it is not the entire Ethiopian Bible and should not be presented as a CSB translation.

These are original study summaries and prompts, not full reproduced translations. Some works have differing chapter numbering and English editions. Consult an appropriate published text for the passage itself.
