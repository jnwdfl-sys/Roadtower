Downloaded travel areas

Save multiple stops along a route while online (up to 25 areas). Area downloads require internet and may be unavailable if the public data service is busy. No automatic cross-country route download yet; a saved area covers only its selected radius. Offline GPS is not a substitute for road directions or emergency navigation. Road Hazards & Winter Driving
